03 / CULINARY PHILOSOPHY

THE TRIAD OF SMOKE, WOOD & TIME

Every cut of meat represents patient craftsmanship, natural oak fires, and precise thermal control.

01

PROPRIETARY 14-SPICE RUB

Every cut is hand-trimmed and massaged with our house blend of toasted chili, brown sugar, smoked paprika, sea salt, and black peppercorn.

02

12-HOUR HICKORY SMOKE

We burn 100% natural hickory and cherry logs slowly at 225°F for up to 16 hours to cultivate the iconic deep red smoke ring and crisp bark glaze.

03

800°F WOOD FLAME SEAR

Steaks are seared over open white oak fire at 800 degrees to lock in natural marbling juices while caramelizing the seasoned exterior crust.
